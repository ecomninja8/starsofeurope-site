Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Stars of Europe (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates starsofeurope.site. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use this website.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us: if you contact us by email or through our contact form, we receive your name, email address and the contents of your message. We use that information only to reply to you.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools may collect standard log information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the time and date of your visit. This information is used to keep the site secure, to understand which pages readers find useful, and to improve the site. It is not used to identify you personally.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use cookies to keep the site working, to remember basic preferences, and, if advertising is enabled, to show relevant ads. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may then work less well.

3. Google AdSense and third-party advertising

We may use Google AdSense to show advertisements on this website. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Some of our advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons on our site. Our advertising partners include Google. Each partner has its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to read those policies. We have no access to, or control over, the cookies used by third-party advertisers.

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain the website, to reply to your messages, to improve our content, to keep the site secure, and to comply with legal obligations.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the website (for example, hosting, security and advertising providers), and only as needed for them to perform their services, or where required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep contact messages only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. Log data is kept for a limited period for security and analytics purposes and is then deleted or anonymised.

7. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to ask us to correct or delete it, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, contact us at contact@starsofeurope.site. For visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page, with a new “last updated” date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@starsofeurope.site or through our Contact page.
