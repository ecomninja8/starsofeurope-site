The Ranking in Full

1
French Celebrities

Kylian Mbappé

Estimated net worth: $180m–$250m
Photo: Helfer Emilio, Wikimedia Commons, CC0 (public domain)

Kylian Mbappé is, by a wide margin, the richest celebrity on this list — more than double the fortune of second-placed Penélope Cruz. The Real Madrid forward and France captain won the World Cup at 19, became the all-time top scorer in World Cup history with 22 goals at the 2026 tournament, and finished runner-up for the 2026 Ballon d'Or. His wealth comes from a reported Real Madrid salary of around €31 million a year, a signing bonus reported at €100 million–€150 million, and endorsements including On, Hublot and Dior.

2
Spanish Celebrities

Penélope Cruz

Estimated net worth: $75m–$90m
Photo: Georges Biard, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Penélope Cruz is the second-richest celebrity on this list, and the richest actress we have covered so far. She is the first Spanish actress ever to win an Oscar (Best Supporting Actress for Vicky Cristina Barcelona, won in 2009), with four Oscar nominations in total, and Pedro Almodóvar's most frequent muse, with seven films together. Her wealth comes from more than three decades of leading film roles — including Pirates of the Caribbean: On Stranger Tides, which grossed over $1 billion — plus long-running endorsements, above all as a Chanel ambassador since 2018.

French Celebrities

Marion Cotillard

Estimated net worth: $30m–$50m
Photo: Georges Biard, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Marion Cotillard won the Academy Award for Best Actress for La Vie en Rose (2007) and has worked steadily at the top of French and Hollywood cinema ever since — Taxi, Inception, The Dark Knight Rises, Macbeth and Lee among them. She has appeared on more than 300 magazine covers and works with Chopard on ethical jewellery, and her long career at the highest level of two film industries is what the fortune is built on.

Spanish Celebrities

Javier Bardem

Estimated net worth: $30m–$40m
Photo: Sarran Hamilton, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Javier Bardem was the first Spaniard ever to win an acting Oscar — Best Supporting Actor for No Country for Old Men (won February 2008) — and has four Oscar nominations in total. His biggest paydays came from blockbusters like Skyfall (as Silva) and the Dune films (as Stilgar), alongside acclaimed work in Before Night Falls, Biutiful and Being the Ricardos. In 2026 he stars opposite his wife Penélope Cruz in Bunker, their first film together since 2018.

Turkish Celebrities

Can Yaman

Estimated net worth: $8m–$25m
Photo: Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0 IT

Can Yaman built his fortune on Turkish television (Erkenci Kuş, Dolunay, Bay Yanlış) and then on a second career in Italy (Viola come il mare, Sandokan). He is one of the few Turkish actors to become a star in two countries, and his first Spanish series, El laberinto de las mariposas, premieres on 13 November 2026.

6
French Celebrities

Omar Sy

Estimated net worth: $10m–$20m
Photo: Harald Krichel, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0

Omar Sy won the César for Best Actor for The Intouchables (2012) — one of the highest-grossing French films ever — and then became a global star as Assane Diop in Netflix's Lupin, one of the platform's biggest non-English hits. Hollywood followed with X-Men: Days of Future Past and Jurassic World, and in 2022 he led the war drama Father & Soldier (Tirailleurs). His fortune is built on French cinema, streaming and Hollywood, in that order.

7
Turkish Celebrities

Hande Erçel

Estimated net worth: $8m–$10m
Photo: Magazin Burada, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

Hande Erçel is the most-followed Turkish actress on Instagram, with more than 32 million followers — and that audience is a large part of how she earns. Her breakthrough was Güneşin Kızları, and Sen Çal Kapımı (as Eda Yıldız, opposite Kerem Bürsin) made her an international star; Bambaşka Biri then won the Rose d'Or for Best Telenovela in 2023, the first Turkish series ever to do so. Alongside acting, she works with L'Oréal Turkey, Nocturne and, since April 2025, Pomellato as a global ambassador.

8
Turkish Celebrities

Burak Özçivit

Estimated net worth: $5m–$10m
Photo: Wikilife77, Wikimedia Commons, CC0 (public domain)

Burak Özçivit started as a model — Best Model of Turkey 2005, second at Best Model of the World — and became one of the actors who carried Turkish television abroad. Kara Sevda (2015–2017) was sold to more than 100 countries and won the International Emmy for Best Telenovela in 2017, and he then played Osman Gazi for a record six seasons and 194 episodes of Kuruluş: Osman. In 2026 he leads the new ATV drama Güneşin Doğduğu Yer, and he also runs his own production company, BRK's Production.

Turkish Celebrities

Demet Özdemir

Estimated net worth: $1m–$5m
Photo: Tolga Çevik, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

Her earnings come from television leads, films and brand work, including covers for Elle and Madame Arabia.

How the Ranking Works

Every figure on this page is a range from the published estimates used in that celebrity's biography on this site — never a single invented number. No celebrity publishes their bank balance, and no audited figure exists for any of them, so any site that gives you one exact number is guessing. We show the range instead, and we say where it came from.

Where two ranges overlap, the order uses the middle of the range. That is why Can Yaman ($8m–$25m) sits above Omar Sy ($10m–$20m) even though Omar's lower figure is higher: the middle of Can Yaman's range is slightly higher, but the two are effectively close, and the order could change as new estimates appear.

The list only includes celebrities with a full biography on Stars of Europe. That is why it has nine ranked names and one open place today — and why it will look different in a week. When a new biography goes live, its subject joins the ranking in their true place, and the page is updated.

FAQs

Who is the richest European or Turkish celebrity in 2026?

Kylian Mbappé — an estimated $180 million to $250 million, far ahead of second-placed Penélope Cruz ($75m–$90m). His fortune comes from his Real Madrid salary and signing bonus, endorsements including On, Hublot and Dior, and his own business ventures. Read his full biography on Stars of Europe.

Who is the richest Turkish celebrity on the list?

Can Yaman, with an estimated $8 million to $25 million — ahead of Hande Erçel ($8m–$10m), Burak Özçivit ($5m–$10m) and Demet Özdemir ($1m–$5m). His range is the widest on the page, because published estimates about him disagree the most.

Why are the figures ranges instead of exact numbers?

Because no exact number exists. Net-worth websites publish different figures for the same person — for example, estimates for Can Yaman range from $8 million to $25 million. Giving one number would be pretending to know something nobody knows. A range is the honest answer, and every biography on this site explains where its range comes from.

Are these official figures?

No. These are estimates compiled from published sources — see our Disclaimer. No celebrity on this page has published an audited net worth, and none of these figures comes from the celebrities themselves.

Will Kylian Mbappé top the list?

He already has. Since 13 November 2026, Mbappé is number one on this list, with an estimated $180 million–$250 million — more than double the $75m–$90m of second-placed Penélope Cruz. Read his full biography for the details.
