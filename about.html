About Stars of Europe

Stars of Europe is an English-language website about European and Turkish celebrities — beyond Hollywood.

Most celebrity websites focus on Hollywood. We focus on the stars of France, Italy, Spain and Turkey: their biographies, lifestyles, fashion, films and television dramas, and the culture that made them famous at home and abroad.

What we cover

Biography: early life, career, awards and personal stories, written in our own words.

Lifestyle: daily life, family, homes, fitness and public appearances.

Fashion: red-carpet looks, Paris and Milan Fashion Week, and everyday style.

Movies and dramas: filmographies, Turkish dizis loved across Europe, and what to watch next.

How we work

Our articles are original and written in English for a global audience. Where a fact, figure or net-worth estimate comes from public reporting, we treat it as an estimate, not as an official statement. We do not copy articles from other websites, and we do not present rumours as facts.

If you spot a mistake, please tell us through our Contact page and we will check it and correct it.

Stars of Europe is an independent fan and information website. It is not owned by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any celebrity, studio, production company or fashion house mentioned on it.
